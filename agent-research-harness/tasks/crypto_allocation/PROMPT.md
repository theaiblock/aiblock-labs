# One research iteration

Read `MANDATE.md`, `FEEDBACK.md`, and `JOURNAL.md` completely. Inspect `train/prices.csv` and the
snapshot manifest, then inspect the
candidate interface. Choose one untested hypothesis, then write:

1. `proposal.json` with `slug`, `hypothesis`, and `expected_direction`.
2. `candidate.py` implementing `allocate(history)`.

You cannot see the evaluation dataset or evaluator. Do not search outside this workspace. Stop
after producing one submission; the host owns scoring and archival.
