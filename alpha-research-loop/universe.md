Point-in-time top 100 Binance spot coins (USDT pairs) by 30-day average daily volume, recomputed
every day. Minimum 60 days of history and $2M average daily volume. Stablecoins, fiat, leveraged
tokens, wrapped BTC/ETH and tokenised stocks are excluded. Delisted coins are included for the time
they traded. About 460 different coins pass through the universe between 2020 and 2025; at any date
it holds 100 (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE and other large coins, plus whatever small coins are trading
heavily that month).
