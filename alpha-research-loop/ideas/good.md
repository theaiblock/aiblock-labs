Improve the benchmark by sizing positions by risk.

The benchmark gives every coin in an uptrend the same weight, so a small coin that moves 10% a day
takes as much of the portfolio as BTC. Most of the portfolio's risk then comes from its most volatile
holdings. Propose a way to weight each held coin by the inverse of its recent volatility, so every
position contributes a similar amount of risk, while keeping the benchmark's rule for which coins are
held.
