# Pre-run hypothesis

On daily BTC, a long/flat asymmetric Donchian rule that enters when the close reaches or exceeds the prior 120-day high and exits when the close reaches or falls below the prior 60-day low will capture persistent upside trends while exiting prolonged reversals early enough to remain positive after explicit crypto trading costs and fixed funding.

Why this is next: the only tested fingerprint is a slow 50/200-day moving-average rule. Breakouts are an uncovered, materially different momentum family, and the journal specifically identifies 120-day entry / 60-day exit Donchian momentum as the next useful test. This is one fixed, canonical hypothesis rather than a parameter sweep or a nudge to the SMA baseline.
